How to Apply
  1. Register or log in to your account
  2. Browse available job openings
  3. Complete all required application forms
  4. Upload necessary credentials and documents
  5. Submit your application before closing date
  6. Download and print your confirmation slip
⚠ All information submitted must be accurate and verifiable. False declarations attract immediate disqualification and prosecution.

General Requirements

Original certificates must be presented at any verification stage
📄 Academic Qualifications
  • O'level results — WAEC, NECO or NABTEB
  • First degree or HND from accredited institution
  • NYSC Discharge or Exemption Certificate
  • Postgraduate certificates where applicable
  • Professional certificates for technical roles
🪪 Personal Documents
  • Valid National Identity Card (NIN slip)
  • Birth Certificate or Age Declaration
  • Lagos State indigene letter or proof of residency
  • Two recent passport photographs (white background)
  • Letter of good conduct from Local Government
